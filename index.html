System Capabilities

Everything Built For Academics

Clean separation between administrative governance and student self-service portals.

👥

Student Directory & CRUD

Admins can add, view, update, and remove students with Course (B.Tech, BCA, B.Com), Specialization (AI, IoT, CSE), Roll Number, Phone, and Semester.

🔍

Instant Search & Filters

Search any student in real time by Name, Roll Number, Phone, or ID with multi-criteria dropdown filters for Branch and Semester.

📅

Attendance Tracker

Mark daily class presence or absence. Visual circular attendance rings calculate exam eligibility thresholds with a 75% shortage alert.

📊

Marks & Report Cards

Record Internal, Mid-Term, and Final Exam marks. Automatic total aggregate and letter grade computation (A+, A, B, C, F) with CGPA display.

📝

Homework & Tasks

Teachers assign course-specific homework with due dates. Students can track pending tasks and mark them as submitted with 1 click.

🔔

Notices & Toast Alerts

College-wide bulletin board for exam schedules, hackathons, and announcements, supplemented by responsive interactive toast popups.
